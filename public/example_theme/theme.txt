My proposed direction is a combination of:
- Industrial minimalism: Clean, structured layouts and strong typography.
- Engineering aesthetics: Technical grids, diagrams, measurements and schematic-inspired details.
- Motorsport energy: Bold headlines, oversized numbers and dynamic imagery.
- Modern technology: Subtle animations, sophisticated interactions and carefully designed components.
I specifically want to avoid:
- Generic AI-generated-looking websites.
- Excessive neon blue and purple gradients.
- Too many glassmorphism cards.
- Unnecessary animations everywhere.
- A typical Bootstrap-style layout.

Design principles for implementation:
- Responsive from mobile to desktop.
- Reusable components.
- Smooth scrolling and restrained motion.
- Excellent image optimisation.
- Accessible contrast and keyboard navigation.
- Fast loading, without unnecessary animation libraries or effects.

Motion and animation system
The website should feel alive without feeling like a game.










Hero entrance

Headline reveals in short staggered sequences. The robot image fades in with a subtle upward movement.













Scroll reveal

Sections appear gently as the user scrolls. No exaggerated zooming or bouncing.













Statistics

Verified achievement numbers can animate once when entering the viewport.













Image interactions

Very subtle image transitions, with optional gallery navigation.
